# Email drafter

1. Establish: who it is to, what they know, what you want from them and by when, and the tone (formal, friendly, firm).
2. Write: a specific subject; first line states the purpose or the ask; context in two or three short sentences; the ask repeated with a date; a plain sign-off. Under 150 words unless the content needs more.
3. For a reply, quote nothing extra, answer every question asked, and keep the thread's language and formality.
4. Give the draft and, only if useful, one alternative with a different tone. Never send anything; the user does that.
